GET TO KNOW ME

About Me.

Creativity Meets Technology.

I'm Ridma Chamuditha, a passionate Drone Pilot, Aerial Videographer, Digital Marketer and Content Creator based in Sri Lanka. I'm currently pursuing a degree in Data Science at SLTC Research University.

My journey into drone technology began in 2016, when I started learning drone piloting. In 2019, I began working on drone videography projects, transforming my passion into creative visual storytelling.

In 2024, I completed a Drone Pilot Course at KDU and joined Kenilworth International as a Drone Pilot and Digital Marketer, where I worked until June 27, 2025.

Since June 2025, I've been working as a Freelance Digital Marketer, Drone Videographer and Content Creator, delivering aerial filming, creative content and social media solutions for various clients.

I combine creativity, technology and hands-on experience to bring unique perspectives to every project.
